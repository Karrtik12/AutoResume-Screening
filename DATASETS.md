# Open Resume Datasets Reference

This document summarizes high-quality open-source datasets available on Kaggle and Hugging Face for resume screening, parsing, and candidate-job matching.

---

## 📌 Kaggle Datasets

1. **[AI-Driven Resume Screening Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/)**
   - High-quality synthetic dataset replicating modern AI-assisted recruitment workflows with candidate attributes, job specifications, and labels.
2. **[Resume Screening Dataset (200K Candidates)](https://www.kaggle.com/)**
   - Large-scale dataset containing 200,000 resume records designed for training deep learning and transformer-based recruitment models.
3. **[Resume Screening Dataset 4500](https://www.kaggle.com/)**
   - 4,500 candidates with pre-parsed education, skill tags, and experience metrics.
4. **[AI-Powered Resume Screening Dataset (2025)](https://www.kaggle.com/)**
   - Over 1,000 synthetic resumes paired with recruiter decision labels and AI screening scores.

---

## 📌 Hugging Face Datasets

1. **[datasetmaster/resumes](https://huggingface.co/datasets/datasetmaster/resumes)**
   - Advanced Resume Parser & Job Matcher collection containing merged real and synthetic JSON-formatted resumes normalized to a common schema.
2. **[0xnbk/resume-ats-score-v1-en](https://huggingface.co/datasets/0xnbk/resume-ats-score-v1-en)**
   - ATS semantic scoring dataset with resume-job description pairs and normalized match scores.
3. **[Akhil-Theerthala/Resume-Analysis-CoTR](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Akhil-Theerthala/Resume-Analysis-CoTR)**
   - Chain-of-thought analysis and qualitative feedback for resume evaluation.
